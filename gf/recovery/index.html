Polling (G130, the owner, 2026-11-11). Poll first shows to recovery.confirm holders and calls POST loans/{id}/poll/ (loans.follow_up, which every confirm holder has). Confirming still answers "poll first" while the loan hasn't been polled in the last hour.

RC1 · Recovery (note 28, GET cases/). Every attempt to recover a defaulted loan through the member's cooperative. A case is queued by itself when a loan defaults (26), and waits here to confirm: GroFunder checks the loan before anything is withheld from a member's pay (21 Q6). Then the cooperative agrees an arrangement with the member (to agree), and the payouts carry the deductions (recovering).

Confirming (step 3). In API mode the loan is polled first, so the figures are the lender's latest; the button says so until it has been. A case can only be confirmed where the cooperative has collection terms in force that recover and are acknowledged: Kijani's are, Mawingu's aren't acknowledged, Ruiru's say it doesn't recover, and Gatundu has none. Confirming and declining need recovery.confirm (Full and Owner).

A cure while waiting withdraws a pending case by itself: the loan came back up to date, so nothing is recovered.
