K6 · Visit (note 20, signed off 2026-11-07). The agent's checklist for one member, phone-first. Each row opens a panel for that kind of information; each farm is its own row, so a farm's panel (K7) opens straight from here and never from another panel (UI-13).

What submitting needs (note 20, OPEN → SUBMITTED): ID, portrait and phone checked on this visit; background and next of kin present (calling next of kin checks it, but isn't needed to submit); every farm checked, or a note saying why not; and the tick for going through the declared details. Crops, other income and loans are gone through too, but don't hold up submitting: lending checks them later. Home is optional.

What the agent sees of the member (note 20, agents and permissions): name, phone, cooperative, cluster and home area, and nothing else from the cooperative's record. So the ID panel doesn't show the record's ID: the agent types what the document shows, and the office compares the two when the visit is submitted.

Verified or declared. Identity, portrait, phone, farms and crops are checked here, and next of kin by calling them (G65, 2026-11-08). Each check counts once the office approves the visit, for 12 months. Background, other income and loans can't be verified on a visit: they stay "Declared", but the agent goes through them with the member and corrects what's wrong (the tick in the footer, notes.went_through_declared).

Who writes (audit, 2026-11-08): only the visit's own agent (kyc.capture on visits assigned to them). Office roles, and an agent on someone else's visit, see it read only. The agent can confirm an ID, portrait or phone already on file (Matches) or save what changed, photograph it again, End an item that no longer holds (a farm sold, a loan paid off) with a reason, Correct a crop, note why a crop wasn't seen, and note the next-of-kin call.
