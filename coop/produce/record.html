C4 · From note 07 (UI-30), phone-first (UI-2). Clerks record one delivery after another at the collection centre. The date, round and centre rarely change in a session, so they are set once at the top and kept. Each entry is then the member, the quantity and the receipt, with the grade where the product is graded. That makes it three fields and one tap.

Hard rules are field errors: an exited member, a quantity of 0, a receipt number already on a recorded delivery. Soft checks warn and never block, because late entries and one-off collections are normal: a day the round doesn't run, a round for another product or cluster, a second delivery in the same round. No price is needed to record.

The recording unit. At Mawingu maize is typed in bags and the stored kilograms show live beneath it (QuantityEntry, F6). The entry keeps the factor used, so a later change of factor doesn't rewrite it.

After Record the form clears the member, quantity and receipt, keeps the rest, and the member field takes focus again. The entry joins "Recorded here today" below, and its total is the figure clerks check against the cans or the scale.

F5 · offline. Collection centres often have poor signal. The offline state shows entries queued on the phone. It needs a kit decision on offline writes.
