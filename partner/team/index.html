PP6 · Team (note 29, "The surface" and "Roles and permissions"). Your staff are members of Chemi Chemi Sacco's account, invited and managed with the kit's team screens, as a cooperative's staff are. The account's owner manages the team; everyone else reads it.

The four roles (Q4). Administrator: everything, and the only one who may write a loan off. Credit officer: the queue and decisions. Finance: disbursements, payments and money. Viewer: reads only. There is no two-person rule inside the partner in v1; your own controls apply. A partner with many staff should give most of them Viewer or Credit officer only (concern 2).

Two-step sign-in is required for every partner staff login (Q5), because staff see members' details and record money. Every opening of a member's details is logged with the staff member's name.
