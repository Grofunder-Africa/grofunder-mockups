CR5 · A rules version (note 25, ScoringRuleSet). The factors with their weights and settings, the limit ladder, and how old a score may be before the app works it out again. Weights are 0–100 and add up to 100; a factor at 0 is recorded only (declared income, v1). The ladder sets the limit by score alone (Q3) and rises in both limit and minimum score.

Draft (credit.publish): edit, preview, publish or delete. Preview works out up to 50 members' scores under the draft and under the version in force, writing nothing, so staff see what a change does first. Publish checks the schema, the weights, that every factor is registered in code, the rising ladder (minimums 300–850), and a start now or later at a moment no other published version uses. A scheduled version can be withdrawn with a reason (C2); once in force, the fix is a new version.

Audit, 2026-11-08: the draft's sections have visible Edit buttons. Factors can be added (registered in code, with their default settings) or removed. Saving checks each value; weights not totalling 100 or a ladder that doesn't rise only warn, since publishing checks them. A draft holds when it takes effect (ruling 1); publishing uses it, or "take effect now". Preview any members (up to 50) or a whole cooperative. Any version can be the start of a new draft.

Editing a draft (2026-11-08): weights and every factor's settings, the refresh time, the ladder (add and remove steps) and the notes, each checked as the service checks them. When it takes effect is set when publishing (a6 is reviewing whether a draft should hold it before then).
