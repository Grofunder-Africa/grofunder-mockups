A clickable mockup of GroFunder's three apps, grouped by the journeys people take through them. Each flow says who it's for and what it's for, then lists its pages: what each one is about and what you can do there. Every name and figure is fictional.

How to read it

Review bar
The dark bar at the top of every page is the prototype's, not the app's. Press H to hide it.
Role
Switches what the role can see. Content a role can't use is removed, not greyed out (note 09). "Try" links marked "as …" set the role or the cooperative's status before opening the page; the choice stays until you change it.
Cooperative
In the portal and the member app: onboarding, active, on hold or ended, and what changes with each (note 01).
State
Every page's loading, empty, error and loaded states; a flow's steps are states too. ?state= opens one directly.
Notes
Why the page is the way it is. A red count is gaps: things the design needs that don't exist yet.
Widths
Under 600 is a phone (bottom bar), 600 to 1199 a rail, 1200 and up an expanded rail.
