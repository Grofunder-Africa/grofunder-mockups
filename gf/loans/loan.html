LN3 · Loan (note 26 part 1). Everything a decision rests on: the request and the quote as accepted, the checks run again now with a fresh score, the score and any override, the KYC standing and visit, the circle and its borrowing spaces, the re-confirmed declarations, the consents the acceptance wrote, every review and the status history.

Reviewing (Q2, Q7). Each product sets how many different people approve (copied onto the loan when the member accepts). Each approval runs the checks again; the last one needed makes it APPROVED and records what it relied on. Anyone may reject, with a reason, which ends it. Whoever prepared the draft, and the borrower, can't review; nor can someone who already approved. The amount can't be changed: to offer less, reject and prepare a new draft. If a check fails now, Approve is refused and the reason is prefilled.

A draft waits 7 days for the member. Without the app, staff send a code whose SMS carries the key figures, read the quote out from the script on screen, and enter the code the member reads back; the quote is worked out again from the terms in force first. Approved loans go to the lender with the gateway (L4): shown as the next step only. approved shows Samuel's loan after its second approval.

G110: note 26 lets staff cancel a draft or a request (BY_GROFUNDER) but names a code only for cancelling after approval (loans.manage). Drawn: a draft by loans.prepare, a request by loans.review.
