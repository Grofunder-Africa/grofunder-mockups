M15 · Change PIN (note 06). The kit's password change: the current PIN, then the new one twice. The kit ends every other session, so other phones are signed out. One step per screen on the keypad, as at sign-in.

The PIN rule (06, MemberPinValidator): exactly 4 digits, refusing one digit repeated (0000), a run up or down (1234, 9876) and a repeated pair (1212). Try 1234 on the new PIN.

F4. PinPad and the PIN dots are proposed components (specimens/new-components.html), not yet in the kit.
