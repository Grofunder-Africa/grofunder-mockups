Pattern sample: a task form, phone-first (phase 2). Clerks record deliveries on phones at the collection centres (UI-2), so the form is one column, the main action is pinned at the foot, and the keyboard opens on numbers for the quantity. The produce flow (phase 7) designs it properly from note 07.

A flow, not a destination. No navigation bar under a form being filled in; the app bar has a way back. On a phone that is the whole screen.

One after another. After "Record", the form stays open for the next member and keeps the date, round and centre. The result is a snackbar with View, never a dialog.

Soft checks warn, they don't block (note 07): a second delivery in the same round, a date outside the round's days. Hard rules (an exited member, a quantity of 0) are field errors.

F5: offline. Collection centres often have poor signal, and the kit has only an offline banner. The "offline" state shows what a queued entry could look like; it needs a design and a kit decision.
