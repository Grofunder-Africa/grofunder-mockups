C18 · From note 08 (UI-31). Every recorded delivery up to the date with a balance is offered, each set to its full balance. Staff lower any amount, or untick a delivery to leave it out; what's left stays as that delivery's balance for a later payout. A delivery paid in part before shows what was paid then, and keeps the price fixed at its first payment.

Three ways to fill, one result (08, recommendations for the screens). Full pays each selected delivery's balance; Percentage pays that share of each; Amount spreads a sum over the selected deliveries oldest first. All three only write per-delivery amounts, and any one amount can still be changed. Above its balance is a field error.

A page, not a panel (UI-13): it is long and has to survive a refresh. A draft is a record, so what is changed here is kept as it's typed; nothing is fixed until Mark as paid (C19). Open Wanjiku's new draft: up to 30 Oct, with half of 15–23 Oct paid before. Or an existing draft: po-9.

Pay to (08): M-Pesa defaults to the member's phone and can be changed for this payout; bank takes the bank and the account's last four digits. The method is chosen here beside where the money goes, so the Mark as paid dialog only asks for the reference and the date.

G35, G36. Where a draft keeps its method and pay-to before it is paid (the model's method isn't nullable), and whether a saved draft's date or product can change, or it is cancelled and started again. Until answered, Change shows only before the draft is first saved.

Who (09): a Viewer reads the draft with nothing to change; Accountant, Manager and Administrator work on it.
