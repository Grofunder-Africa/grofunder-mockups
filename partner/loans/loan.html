PP3 · A loan (note 29, "The loan through the portal"; 26 part 2 for the ledger). The application and the quote the member accepted, the terms version, the schedule GroFunder generated from the terms and your disbursement date, every payment you posted with the split GroFunder worked out, the charges, and the status history. What you record here raises the canonical event beside it; GroFunder's ledger does the rest.

What you can do, by role. Record a disbursement, or one that failed (partner_portal.disburse: Administrator, Finance). Record a payment, or reverse one with a reason while the loan isn't closed or written off (partner_portal.payments). Waive a late penalty, with a reason (the same). Write off a defaulted loan, with a reason and the amount (partner_portal.write_off: the Administrator alone, Q4). A payment above the balance is refused: refund the member and record only what was owed. There is no restructuring in v1 (Q6): ask GroFunder.

How a payment is split (Q3): oldest instalment first and, within it, its fees, then interest, then principal; open late penalties only once every instalment due is paid; anything more pays ahead. GroFunder splits it, because its ledger is the record.
