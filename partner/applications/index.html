Cancelling (G142, the owner, 2026-11-11). Both may cancel before disbursement, each under its own rule: the lender here, under partner_portal.decide (BY_PARTNER); GroFunder in the console, under loans.manage (BY_GROFUNDER), which for a portal lender makes no outside call and sends it an "application withdrawn" notice.

Member details (G140, the owner). A loan is never forwarded without sharing consent. After a withdrawal, a live loan keeps access; once the lender's loans are final, opening the details is refused and logged, and the panel says "Not shared", with the name and the lender's own loans. ?notshared=1 on a loan shows it.

PP1 · Applications (note 29, "The loan through the portal", steps 1 and 2). Loans GroFunder has approved on your products. For a portal lender, forwarding makes no outside call: GroFunder raises application.received itself and the loan appears here. You see the request, the quote and the terms version it was quoted on, and the date GroFunder approved it — never who approved it, the member's credit score or GroFunder's review notes.

Deciding (partner_portal.decide: Administrator and Credit officer). Approve takes the application as asked for: a portal lender can't approve a different amount (Q2). To lend less, reject with a note or ask for an amendment, and GroFunder prepares a new draft for the member to accept. Ask for an amendment sends it back to GroFunder, which answers with corrected details and returns it here. Cancel is allowed before disbursement, with a reason. Each of these raises its canonical event with your name on it.
