C10 · From note 05 and its draft services (UI-35). Staff add members one at a time; there is no self-application. An ID type and number are required. The ID is checked against the register as it is typed, because one record per person per cooperative is the rule: (cooperative, id_type, id_number) is unique. An ID already in the register names that member and links to them, as the service's existing_member answer allows.

Normalised as typed (05). A national ID loses spaces and a trailing ".0" from a spreadsheet, and must be 5–12 digits. A passport or alien ID loses spaces and is upper-cased, 5–20 letters and digits. A phone in a local spelling is stored as +254…

A shared phone is fine. Family members often share one (05, concern 1), so a phone already on the register is a note naming who has it, never an error.

Member number: suggested as the next after the highest in use (suggest_member_no), and still checked: a clash is an error on the field. With no numbered member yet, it's blank and the help says so (the empty state).

Nominees are optional, up to five. Each needs a name and a share, and the shares add up to 100; the running total says how far off they are.

Status gating (01, 05): adding is refused while the cooperative is on hold or ended (accepts_new_work), so the form gives way to the reason. While onboarding it's allowed: loading members is part of setting up. Only Administrators and Managers add members (members.manage).

After Add the form clears for the next member, keeping the cluster, home area and date joined, because members are usually added a cluster at a time. The snackbar opens the new record. In this mockup, Open goes to an existing member as a stand-in.
