C44 · Collection terms (note 28, step 1; GET cooperatives/{id}/recovery/terms/). GroFunder agrees these with the cooperative outside the system and enters them as a dated version. The cooperative's administrator acknowledges them. No case opens under terms that aren't in force, don't recover, or aren't acknowledged (Q8). Published terms never change: a change is a new version.

Acknowledging (recovery.acknowledge) is the Administrator's alone, and happens once per version. Kijani's version 1 is acknowledged; toack shows the screen as it is before that, which is also what Mawingu sees today.

The most one payout may give up caps every arrangement, including the collection fee: the loan and the fee together never take more than that share of a payout.
