LN7 · Flags (note 26 part 2, LoanFlag). Everything a lender hasn't told us, or that doesn't add up, raised by forwarding, the gateway's handler, the poll and the count: forwarding failed, the approved figures differ from the quote, disbursement failed or estimated, a schedule changed without a restructure, the poll found a difference, an event against the status rules, a payment's split unknown, past the flag threshold, an event failed. One open flag of a kind per loan; a repeat adds to it.

Who. Every office role reads. Operations and up (loans.follow_up, Q9) resolve a flag, with an optional note, or dismiss it, with a note. The system also closes a flag when its cause clears (a split arrives, the lender defaults the loan). Nothing is edited or deleted.

Amendment requests aren't flags: they are loans waiting in With the lender (LN1), answered on the loan.
