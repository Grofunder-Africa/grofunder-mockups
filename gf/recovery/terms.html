RC3 · Collection terms (note 28, GET cooperatives/{id}/terms/). What each cooperative may take from a member's pay, what it earns for collecting, and how often it remits. GroFunder agrees the terms outside the system and enters them as a dated version; the cooperative's administrator acknowledges them in its portal.

No terms, no case. A cooperative with nothing in force, or whose terms say it doesn't recover, never receives a case: its defaulted loans are GroFunder's to settle with the lender. Terms that are in force but not acknowledged stop a case being confirmed until the administrator acknowledges them (Q8).

Versions work as a lender's terms do (24): one draft at a time, published from a date, never changed once published, and withdrawn only before they take effect. Drafting and publishing need recovery.terms (Full and Owner).
