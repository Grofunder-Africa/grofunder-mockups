C25 · From note 05 (Position). Every new cooperative starts with the default list (chair, vice chair, secretary, treasurer, board member, supervisory committee member, cluster leader), which it renames, extends or archives. Editing needs members.configure (Administrator and Manager).

The rules on screen (05). Scope can't change while anyone holds the position; the limit can't go below the people holding it now (per cluster for a cluster position); a position never held can be deleted, otherwise it is archived and its past holders keep their record. Names are unique, archived or not.

Page, panel, dialog (UI-13). A position opens as a panel with its holders; Add is a dialog of four fields. Who holds what is assigned on a member's record (phase 6), so holders here are read-only.
