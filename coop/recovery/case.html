C41 · A recovery case (note 28; GET cooperatives/{id}/recovery/cases/{id}/). What the member owes, what was agreed, the two charges on their payouts (the loan first, the fee only once the loan is recovered, Q1), every withholding by payout, and what is still to remit to GroFunder.

Recording the arrangement (recovery.agree, Administrator and Manager). FULL takes as much of each payout as the terms allow; PARTIAL takes a fixed amount or a share of each payout. Anything above the terms' maximum is refused, naming it. The date agreed can't be in the future, and how it was agreed is kept as evidence. The fee is worked out once, when the arrangement is first recorded, and never again (Q2). The member is told in the app and by SMS.

Never shown here: the loan's terms or charges, the lender's pricing, and the member's credit score. The cooperative sees the loan's reference, the lender's name and the debt only.
