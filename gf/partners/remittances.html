LP7 · Remittances (note 24, PartnerRemittance; GET remittances/, remittances/totals/). What each partner has paid GroFunder of its share, by the period of collections it covers. Staff record each one from the bank statement (Q6), with settle (Full and Owner); from L4 the partner's share.remitted event records it. A mistake is voided with a reason, never deleted or edited.

What a partner owes isn't here yet. It is worked out by loans (26) from each payment's split and each charge's GroFunder share, and set beside these totals then. In part 1 the console shows what was remitted, by period, only.

Recording checks: received today or earlier; a reference not already recorded for this partner (allowed again once the first is voided); a breakdown, if given, adds up to the amount and names charges on the partner's published terms.
