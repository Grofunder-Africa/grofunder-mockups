K1 · Verifications (note 20, signed off 2026-11-07). One row per visit: an agent's check of one member record, and a reviewer's decision. It opens on To review, the visits agents have submitted, oldest first, because that's the office's daily work. Filters: cooperative and agent; search by member name or number.

Who does what (note 20): View only reads (kyc.view). Operations and up open verifications and assign agents (kyc.assign), and approve, send back or fail (kyc.review), never on a visit they did themselves. Farmer Support Agents don't see this page: their navigation is My visits.

Where visits come from (note 20 Q3): staff open one here (K2) or from the member's panel on their cooperative's page (G10). Assigning an agent to a complete Growth Circle also opens one, with that agent, for each of its members who isn't verified (note 23). Those visits record the circle (Verification.circle, G64 closed 2026-11-08): the row names it, and the circle filter lists one circle's visits.

The cooperative's status (audit, 2026-11-08): a visit can be opened while the cooperative is onboarding or active; not while it's on hold (nothing new starts) or ended (read only).

One member record (note 20 Q1). A visit verifies one cooperative's record. A person who belongs to two cooperatives has two records, each verified on its own.

Warnings are the soft checks worked out when the agent submits (note 20): the ID differing from the cooperative's record, the same ID number under another name in another cooperative, a reused photo, a farm point near another member's verified farm, photos far from the farm or the visit's day, a declared size far from what the agent saw, and every declared item entered by the agent.
