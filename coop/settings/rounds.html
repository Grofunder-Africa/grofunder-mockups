C26 · From note 07 (CollectionRound). Rounds are optional: when and where the cooperative collects. A round serves every cluster or one, and every product or one; the days say how often (all seven is daily), and months make it seasonal. Editing needs produce.configure (Administrator and Manager).

Keeping past deliveries right (07). A big schedule change is a new round: archive the old one, so past deliveries keep pointing at the round as it was. A round never used can be deleted. Names are unique within the cooperative and cluster, archived or not.

Page, panel, dialog (UI-13). A round has seven fields, so adding and editing happen in a panel over the list.
