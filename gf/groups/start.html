GC3 · Start a circle for members (note 23 Q6; POST circles/, groups.manage). For members without the app: staff name the leader, the circle and its meeting, and invite at least 4 others from the leader's cluster. The leader's place starts as LEADER and MEMBER; the invitees answer in the app or with an SMS code read to staff (GC2).

UI-93: the meeting can carry an optional map point, typed (staff aren't usually at the meeting); both coordinates or neither, as in MC2.

Who can lead or be invited: ACTIVE members with no current circle, of a cooperative that takes new work (not on hold or ended). The cluster comes from the leader. Starting cancels the leader's own open invitations elsewhere (gap 3). The name is unique in the cooperative among circles not dissolved, ignoring case.
