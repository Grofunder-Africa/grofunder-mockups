M2 · Sign in (note 06). The ID number (its type picked first) or the phone, then the 4-digit PIN on a keypad, then a 6-digit SMS code, every time. The code is sent only after a correct PIN. One step per screen; the steps are this page's states.

One answer for every failure (06): a wrong ID, a wrong PIN, and a login whose records have all ended get the same message, so the screen reveals nothing. After 5 wrong tries the ID is locked for 15 minutes.

Codes (06): 5 minutes, 5 tries, another after 60 s, at most 5 an hour.

F1 and F4. The kit has no ID-or-phone, PIN and SMS-code sign-in (F1), and no PinPad or OtpInput (F4; proposed in specimens/new-components.html).
