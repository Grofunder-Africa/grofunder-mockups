C8 · From note 07 (UI-30). A dated price per product, and per grade where a grade has its own. The price for a day is the latest one that starts on or before it, so each section leads with the price in force today and any future price. Everyone reads prices (produce.view). Managers and administrators set them (produce.configure). Members see the price in force in the member app.

Typed per bag, kept per kg. Where the cooperative records in its own unit (Mawingu's maize, 90 kg bags), the price can be typed per bag. It is stored per catalog unit, and the table shows both. A price that doesn't divide evenly is rounded to the cent, and the dialog says so.

Fixed once paid (08). A price that has valued a paid delivery can't be corrected, so its row has no Correct button and says why. A new dated price is the way to change what members are paid from then on.

Add price is on each product's section, because a price always belongs to one product. The section is where the person is looking, so the product never has to be picked (UI-13).

No prices yet Deliveries can be recorded without a price; their value is worked out once one is set. A payout can't be marked paid until every delivery in it has one.
