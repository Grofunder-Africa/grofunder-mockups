What GroFunder holds about you

Your cooperatives keep your membership records: your name and ID number as on your ID, your phone, your cluster, your deliveries and your payments. GroFunder keeps them for your cooperatives, and shows them to you here.

Who sees it

Each cooperative's staff see only that cooperative's records. You see every record you have linked to your login. Your map position is never shown to other members.

Your login

You sign in with your ID number or phone, your PIN, and a code we send by SMS. Keep your PIN to yourself. Each person needs their own phone number.
