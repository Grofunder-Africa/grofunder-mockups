LN4 · Pauses (note 26, LendingPause). A pause stops new drafts, requests, accepting a draft and approving, for everyone, a cooperative, a cluster or a circle. Requests already waiting stay until it ends, or are rejected or cancelled; approved loans still go to the lender, and later stages carry on. Members see the pause's message in English or Swahili; staff see the reason. One pause per target at a time. Lenders and products are paused on their own pages (24), not here.

Who (Q6). Full and Owner pause and end a cooperative, cluster or circle (loans.pause); only the Owner pauses everyone (loans.pause_all). Pauses are never edited: end one and start another.
