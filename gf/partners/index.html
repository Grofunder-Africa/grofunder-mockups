Who does what (note 24): every office role reads (lending_partners.view), never secrets. Full and Owner add and edit partners, products, drafts and coverage, and activate, pause and end partners (manage); publish terms (publish, Q1); and record remittances (settle). Only the Owner touches connections and their secrets (credentials).
