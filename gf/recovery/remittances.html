RC5 · Cooperative remittances (note 28, steps 7 and 8). What each cooperative sent GroFunder of the loan money it withheld. The cooperative records it on its terms' cycle, with the transfer's reference; GroFunder matches it to the bank or M-Pesa statement and confirms it, or disputes it with a note. Only the loan withholdings are remitted: the collection fee stays with the cooperative.

Confirming makes the pass-ons. One per loan in the remittance, waiting to be sent to the lender (RC6). Confirming and disputing need recovery.receive (Full and Owner).

Withheld but not remitted is what has been taken from paid payouts and no live remittance covers yet. A voided remittance frees its withholdings to be sent again.
