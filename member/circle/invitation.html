MC3 · Invitation (note 23, GET circle-invitations/, accept and decline). The circle's name, leader, meeting and everyone already in or invited, and the days left (14 in all). Accepting vouches for them (Q1): you say you know these people. You're then joining for 5 days, while the others may object; with no objection you become a member.

Accepting needs an ACTIVE membership with no circle, in a cooperative taking new work; it withdraws your other open invitations. Members without the app answer through GroFunder with an SMS code (GC2).

Suspended (G74, decided 2026-11-08): a member whose cooperative has suspended them sees the app as usual with a "suspended" banner, but can't accept (declining is still possible). See suspended.
