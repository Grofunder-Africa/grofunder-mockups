C40 · Recovery (note 28, "How it works"; GET cooperatives/{id}/recovery/cases/). A member's loan has defaulted, and GroFunder has confirmed it. The cooperative agrees with the member how much of each payout goes to the loan, records it, and from then on every payout draft carries the deduction by itself. The cooperative keeps its collection fee and remits the loan money to GroFunder.

What the cooperative never sees (28, Objectives): the loan's terms, the lender's pricing, and any credit score. Only the loan's reference, the lender's name, the debt and what is being taken.

Who (28, Permissions). Administrator, Manager, Accountant and Viewer read recovery (recovery.view); a Clerk has no Recovery in the navigation. Only an Administrator or Manager records an arrangement (recovery.agree).

A case waiting to be agreed is called out at the top. GroFunder reminds the cooperative after 14 days with nothing agreed, then weekly (recovery_daily).

The story: Kijani recovers for Tegemeo Microfinance. Peter Ndungu is recovering at 30% of each payout; Muthoni Thuku's loan is repaid and only the fee is left; Peter Gathogo's case is still with GroFunder; Kamau Nduati's was declined.
