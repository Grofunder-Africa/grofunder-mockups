CN3 · Version (note 22; the kit's admin/versions/{id}/). One version's wording, one language at a time: title, summary (what the app shows first), body, the change note members see when asked again, whether it asks them to agree again, and when it was in force. Published wording is fixed: a database guard refuses any change to it (22, Database guards), and its content hash shows any edit afterwards.

A draft is edited by Full and Owner (consents.manage). Publishing is the owner's alone (consents.publish, Q7): it puts English and Swahili in force together, now or from a date (a notice period), and is refused while one language has no draft (concern 4). A scheduled version can be cancelled; one in force can be taken down, which brings back the one before; a past one can be archived.

Try: ?d=cd-lending&n=2 (an English draft; publishing is refused until the Swahili is written), ?d=cd-terms&n=2 (in force, asks members again), ?d=cd-loan&n=2 (scheduled for 1 Dec), ?d=cd-lending&n=1 (in force; taking it down leaves nothing).
