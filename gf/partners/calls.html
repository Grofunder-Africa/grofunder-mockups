LP9 · Gateway calls (note 24 part 2, PartnerCall; note 27, "Outbound"). Every attempt at an operation we sent a partner: SUBMIT, AMEND, CANCEL, UPDATE_CUSTOMER, POST_CHARGE, REPORT_REPAYMENT, the READ_* operations, READ_ACCOUNT and TEST. It answers: what did we send, when, and what did the partner say? Every outbound operation uses this one log (26, forwarding).

Retries reuse the key. Each attempt is one row; a retry keeps the same idempotency key and counts up the attempt (forwarding retries 5 times over about an hour, 26). The outcome is blank exactly while the call is in flight; once it has finished, a trigger keeps the row fixed.

Redacted. ID images and secrets are never stored, ID numbers are masked to their last 3 digits, and the phone is kept (it is how the partner reaches the member). Bodies are cut to 64 KB.

Pruning. The poll's reads (READ_*) that found nothing new are pruned after 90 days; reads that changed something, or failed, are kept, as is every write, as long as its loan.
