PP2 · Loans (note 29, "Screens"). Every loan on your products, by where it stands. GroFunder's ledger is the record for a portal lender, so the balance, the arrears and each instalment's state are worked out there from what you record here. You see your own loans only: never another lender's anything.

Where a loan goes: waiting for you (forwarded), approved and waiting for the money, being repaid, behind, defaulted (GroFunder's recovery), repaid, rejected, cancelled or written off. Arrears and default are set by GroFunder's nightly sweep, not by you.
