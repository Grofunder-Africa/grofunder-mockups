C30 · The cooperative's notification settings, from note 01. The status notices that reach the cooperative's staff, each by email and in the app. The cooperative sets them for everyone (notification-settings/, cooperatives.edit_profile: administrators and managers); each person can still change their own in Your account. Each switch saves on its own, one tap (UI-13).

Locked notices. "Put on hold" and "ended" are mandatory in note 01, because they change what staff can do, so their switches are shown on and locked. The rest say whether they follow GroFunder's default or the cooperative's own choice.
