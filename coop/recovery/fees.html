C43 · Collection fees (note 28; GET cooperatives/{id}/recovery/fees/). What the cooperative earns for collecting. The fee is worked out once, when the arrangement is first recorded, by the collection terms in force then (Q2). It is taken from the member's payouts only after the loan itself is recovered (Q1), and it is never remitted: the cooperative keeps it.

A fee outlives its case. When a loan is repaid or the case is closed, the fee charge stays open until it is paid off from later payouts. GroFunder may cancel it with a reason, for example after a dispute.

Read-only: nothing is changed here. The amounts come from the fee deductions on paid payouts.
