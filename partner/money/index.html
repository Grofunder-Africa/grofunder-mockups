PP5 · Money (note 29, "Money between the partner and GroFunder"). What you have out with members and what has come back, worked out from GroFunder's ledger: disbursed, collected, outstanding, and what is in arrears. Then GroFunder's share, worked out from the charges your terms set and the payments you have posted (26, "Settlement").

Remittances. You record each transfer you send GroFunder: the amount, the period, the reference and a breakdown. It stays waiting for GroFunder until GroFunder staff see the money and confirm it (Q7; the PENDING status added to 24). A payment whose split GroFunder couldn't work out is left out of the share and listed, so neither side guesses.

Your own records. A portal lender is still the lender: its licence, disclosures and collection practice are its own, so its records export as a CSV for its audits (29, concern 5). G143 (the owner, 2026-11-11): a code of its own, partner_portal.export, held by Administrator and Finance only. The file holds loans (reference, member name, amounts, dates, status, days past due), schedules, payments, charges, remittances, pass-ons and the portal's action log, and no member details beyond the name. Each run is audited.
