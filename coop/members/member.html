C11 · From notes 05, 07 and 08 (UI-35). A member's full record is a page, because it is a place with tabs of its own (design system §3.0). The header holds who they are, their status with a visible Change status, and three figures. The tabs hold the details, deliveries, payouts, positions and history.

C12 · Edit is on each card and opens a panel with that card's fields (UI-13, as G6 does for a cooperative). The ID can't change once the member's login is linked, because the login's username was built from it (05). Phone, preferred name, email and language are also the member's to change in the app. The card says so, and staff can still correct them.

C13 and C14 are dialogs on this page. The status dialog offers only the moves note 05 allows. It asks for a reason where one is needed, and counts the positions a move would end. Assigning a position applies the scope, the member's own cluster and the holder limit, which is counted per cluster, and names who holds a full position.

Try Wanjiku (uses the app, a cluster leader, nominees), Jane, suspended, Daniel, exited, and the chair.

G40 · Unlinking a login. Note 05 says staff unlink a member's login before correcting a wrong ID, and calls unlinking "a phase 5 service". Note 06 doesn't design it: who may do it, what the member is told, and whether the member's other records stay linked. The Identity panel points at it without offering it.
