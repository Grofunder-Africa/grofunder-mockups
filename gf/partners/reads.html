LP10 · Data reads (note 24 part 2, PartnerDataRead; note 27, "Data partners read from us"). Every read of a member's data by a partner, with its service-account key: production records, farm checks, the Growth Circle, loan history, photos. It answers: what did this partner see about this member, when, and on what consent?

Every read is checked and logged, served or refused. It is served only when the partner's sharing consent for that member counts (allows(member, PARTNER_SHARING, partner), with for_existing while servicing a live loan, 22) and the member has a loan forwarded to this partner that is live or ended within the last 90 days. A served read names the consent record it relied on. Otherwise it is refused: no consent, no loan, not found, or a bad request.

Kept as long as the member's consents (22, concern 3), and included in the member's data export. Append-only. Never the agent's name, other members' names, or another partner's name or terms (27 Q7).
