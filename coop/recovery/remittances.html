C42 · Remittances (note 28, steps 7 and 8; GET cooperatives/{id}/recovery/remittances/). What the cooperative withheld for the loans, and sends on to GroFunder on the terms' cycle. The collection fee is never remitted: the cooperative keeps it. GroFunder matches each transfer against its statement and confirms it, or disputes it with a note.

What is still to send comes from the withholdings on paid payouts that no live remittance covers (remittable). Recording one lists each withholding as a line, so every shilling can be traced from the payout to the lender.

Who (28). Administrator, Manager and Accountant record and void (recovery.remit); a Viewer reads. A remittance can be voided while it is waiting or disputed, which frees its withholdings to be sent again; a confirmed one is final.
