RC6 · Pass-ons (note 28, step 9). Money a cooperative recovered, on its way to the lender. One pass-on per loan in a confirmed remittance, under our own reference (<loan>-R<n>): to send when the remittance is confirmed, sent once GroFunder transfers it, reported once the lender is told (REPORT_REPAYMENT, 27), and posted when the lender posts it against the loan, as a payment with channel RECOVERY (26).

Recording the transfer takes the bank or M-Pesa reference and the date; one transfer may cover several pass-ons to the same lender, so they are sent together. It needs recovery.receive (Full and Owner).

A lender that can't take REPORT_REPAYMENT (some lenders today) is paid into its paybill with the loan's reference, and the pass-on stays sent; the poll matches its payment by loan, amount and date, or flags it for staff to link. A pass-on not posted after 7 days is listed for staff.
