LP5 · Terms version (note 24, ProductTerms and TermsCharge). One dated version of a product's terms: the amounts and durations it allows, how it is repaid, when a loan counts as overdue, in default or flagged, and its charges. Loans point at the version they were quoted on, so a published version never changes (a database trigger guards it).

Charges (Q2): every price item, interest included, with what it's worked out on, how often, when the member pays it, an optional floor and cap, and GroFunder's share (gf_share_bps); the partner's share is the rest, so the split always adds up to 100%. The owner's example, 6% interest with 3% to GroFunder, is interest at 6% with GroFunder's share at 50%. The cooperative's collection fee is not a product charge (28).

Drafts (manage) can be edited or deleted. Publishing (publish, Full and Owner, Q1) needs a start now or later (no back-dating), a moment no other published version uses, at most one interest charge and every charge rule met. A scheduled version can be withdrawn with a reason before it starts, freeing its date (P2); once in force, the fix is a new version.

Audit, 2026-11-08: a draft can hold when it takes effect (ruling 1): set or change it on the draft; publishing uses it, or "take effect now", or another moment. Charges on a draft can be reordered and edited by Full and Owner only. Allowed durations must lie between the shortest and the longest. Shamba's draft already holds a date.

Repayment frequency (amendment to note 24, 2026-11-08): publishing is refused unless instalments follow the duration's unit (monthly for durations in months) or the loan is repaid once at the end. A draft that breaks it says so. Version 4 is repaid every two weeks over months.

Every action is on the page (2026-11-09, the owner's review). A draft: Publish (Full and Owner), Edit limits and wording and Edit on those sections, each charge's Edit, Remove and arrows, Add a charge and Delete this draft (manage). A scheduled version: Withdraw before it starts (publish). Any published version: no edits, since published terms never change, but Draft a new version from this one (manage), or Open the draft while one is open (one draft at a time).

Worked example (phase 16, note 26 "The quote"; closes G80). Any version, a draft included, shows what it costs a member for an amount and a duration, from the same function as the member's quote and the reviewer's view: each charge, what the member receives, the instalments, the total repayable and the total cost of credit, late penalties apart. A draft whose repayment doesn't follow the duration's unit can't be worked out, as it can't be published.
